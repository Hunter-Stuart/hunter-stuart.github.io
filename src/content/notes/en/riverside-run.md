I used to run laps on a track — 400 m each, twelve and a half for five kilometres. The numbers were crystal clear, and every lap was identical.

Switching to the riverside:

- Pace went from 6'10" to 5'45" per km — not from trying harder, but from no longer watching the lap count
- Runs per week went from 2.4 to 4.1
- Rain cancels it though: the riverside has no shelter, the track does

## What I took from it

The track's problem was not boredom. It was that **every lap told me how much was left**. Constantly computing remaining distance drains willpower. On the river there is no such readout, attention goes elsewhere, and I end up running longer.

If the goal is consistency rather than record-keeping, removing the counter may beat installing one.
