## Finished

- *Signals and Systems* — a textbook, but actually read cover to cover, two thirds of the exercises done
- *The Design of Everyday Things* — the first time I understood that "hard to use" is a design failure, not personal incompetence
- *In the Midst of Things* — read the local-finance chapters twice
- *West with the Night* — purely for the prose

## Abandoned

- A popular cognitive science book: by 40% it was citing the same handful of papers
- An advanced Python book: everything in it lives in the official docs
- A book on writing: the method was too abstract to turn into an action

## A pattern

All three abandoned books were trying to **make me believe something** rather than teaching me to do something. All four finished ones either applied immediately or were worth reading for the language itself.
