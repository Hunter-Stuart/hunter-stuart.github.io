At the start of October I cleared the desk completely — monitor and keyboard, nothing else. Then I logged a month: daily focus time, and how often I got interrupted.

## Results

| State | Mean focus block | Interruptions per day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cluttered | 42 min | 9 |
| Cleared | 58 min | 5 |

The difference is real, but probably not because of tidiness. Clearing the desk moved my **phone out of sight** — it used to sit right next to the keyboard. The distraction was the device, not the mess.

## So

What actually helped was leaving the phone in another room. A clean desk was a side effect, not the cause. Tidying alone, with the phone still within reach, would likely change nothing.
