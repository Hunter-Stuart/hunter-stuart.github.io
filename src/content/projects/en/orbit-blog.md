## The problem

A personal site tends to collapse into a reverse-chronological list of posts. That structure silently assumes time is the only axis content has. But what I wanted to express is scale — an exam, a course and a project do not sit at the same order of magnitude.

So the axis became **altitude above ground**: six chapters, six heights, from the launch pad out past the Moon.

## How it is built

- **No framework runtime** — paging, theme and art switching are plain TypeScript, inlined into the HTML at build time
- **Two art styles** — ink and oil are two materials, not two shades of one design; `data-art` × `data-theme` yields four palettes
- **Procedural textures** — the paper fibre and oil strokes are generated by hand-written PNG encoding in plain Node, not downloaded assets
- **Inline SVG scenes** — all six orbital scenes are drawn in code; the site ships zero images
