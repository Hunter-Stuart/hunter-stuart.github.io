## Before publishing

- [ ] **`description` is filled in** — it feeds `<meta description>`, `og:description` and the JSON-LD description all at once; empty in one place means empty in all three, and link previews will show no summary
- [ ] **`tags` are filled in** — each becomes an `article:tag`, and they are joined into the JSON-LD `keywords`
- [ ] **`date` is `YYYY-MM-DD`** — becomes `article:published_time` and `datePublished`
- [ ] The filename is lowercase ASCII (keeps URLs clean)

Everything else is automatic: canonical, hreflang, the share image (`/og-image.png`) and the JSON-LD structured data are generated by `BaseLayout` when `type="article"`.
